# Lay out the starting point

Use this skill to tell the user about the starting point or context of a concept, behaviour, situation or problem.

## Principles

**State the initial conditions and context very clearly and completely.** It is impossible to reason about something unless we know where we are starting from: without knowing about its initial conditions and context, we cannot understand how the concept or behaviour proceeds, or what matters the most. State all relevant initial conditions and facts about a problem before explaining or describing it.
