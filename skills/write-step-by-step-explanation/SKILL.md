# Write a step-by-step explanation

Use this skill to write a step-by-step explanation of a concept, behaviour, problem or error. Use the /make-lean-linear-and-coherent skill.

If the explanation involves code, you may wish to use the /clarify-call-stack skill too.

## Principles

**Explain in terms of concepts or behaviours, not low-level implementation details.** Your explanation should flow as a sequence of natural concepts, behaviours or actions, not noisy, low-level implementation details.

**Start with a conceptual skeleton instead of random facts.** Your explanation should start with higher-level concepts that provides a conceptual skeleton which communicates the gist; random, low-level facts are awful at communicating the gist and are out-of-context distractions; only use them to fill in the skeleton once higher-level concepts have been established.
