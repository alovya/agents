# Find the root cause

Use this skill to find and explain the root cause of a behaviour, error or bug.

# Principles

**Go deep: keep asking why and how.** Do not stop at the first explanation that seems plausible or the first thing that looks broken: when some behaviour is hard to understand or something breaks, the immediate user-facing behaviour or error is rarely informative and is rarely the thing that needs fixing or explanation. Instead, starting from the first observed behaviour or error, trace backwards through the chain of events, one event at a time, to find the root cause.

**Highlight the chain of events that led to the behaviour or error.** When explaining the root cause, highlight the slice or chain of events, from start to finish event by event, that led to it: often, something in the middle of the chain is tangled up or has gone awry compared to what is expected, and needs clarification; use the /write-step-by-step-explanation skill.
