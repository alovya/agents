# Find the root cause

Use this skill to find and explain the root cause of a behaviour, error or bug.

# Principles

**Go deep; keep asking why and how.** Do not stop at the first explanation that seems plausible or the first thing that looks broken: when some behaviour is hard to understand or something breaks, the immediate user-facing behaviour or error is rarely informative and is rarely the thing that needs fixing or explanation.

**Highlight the chain of events that led to the behaviour or error.** When explaining the root cause, highlight the slice or chain of events, from start to finish, that led to it: often, something in the middle of the chain is tangled up or has gone awry compared to what is expected, and needs clarification; use the /write-step-by-step-explanation skill.
